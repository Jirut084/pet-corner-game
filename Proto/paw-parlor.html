Paw Parlor

Pick a starter pet, lay out your grooming floor however you like, then open the doors. Carry each pet through the stations its owner asked for and hand it back before their patience runs out.

The Day

  1. Set up. Move stations anywhere on the floor.
  2. Open. Owners arrive and wait at the counter.
  3. Take the order to see what the pet needs.
  4. Carry the pet through every station listed.
  5. Hand it back at the return counter.
  6. Pick a reward once the day's quota is met.

House Rules

Stations run themselves. Drop a pet off and go start another one — up to three owners can be waiting at once.

Put a pet down anywhere. Holding tables and any free wall counter will hold one pet, so your hands are never stuck.

Tips are earned late. Once a pet's list is fully checked off, any extra station it visits before you return it pays a bonus.

The floor locks at opening. Rearranging only happens between days, and the game won't let you wall off a station.
